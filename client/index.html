HOW TO PLAY

💻 DESKTOP:

• A / D or Arrow Keys: Run left/right

• W / Space: Jump (when on ground)

• Shift: Jetpack Boost (hold to fly)

• Mouse: Aim & Left Click to shoot

• R: Reload magazine | 1/2/3: Switch weapons


📱 MOBILE:

• Left thumb joystick: Move & jump

• Right thumb: Aim pad & tap FIRE

• BOOST button: Hold to engage jetpack

• Keep an eye on your jetpack fuel meter!
