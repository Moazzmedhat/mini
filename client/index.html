HOW TO PLAY

💻 DESKTOP:

• A / D or Arrow Keys: Run left/right

• W / Space / Right-Click / Shift: Jetpack Boost & Jump

• Mouse: Aim & Left Click to shoot

• R: Reload | 1 to 6: Quick Weapon Switch


📱 MOBILE:

• Left joystick: Move & push UP to fly with jetpack

• Right thumb: Drag aim zone & tap FIRE

• BOOST button: Hold to engage jetpack

• Tap weapon cart icons below to switch arsenal on the fly
